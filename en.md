# Privacy Policy — AstroClock

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

**AstroClock** ("the app") does not collect, upload to servers or sell personal data. What you type leaves your device only when you send it yourself.

## What the app does with your data
- **No data collection.** The app asks for no account or email, and does not access your location.
- **Fully offline.** All calculations run on your device. The app does not connect to any server.
- **No analytics, ads or trackers.** It includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **Charts you enter.** If you create natal charts, the app stores what you type: a name (any name), the birth date, time and place, and whether one of them is "your chart". They can belong to other people. This data is stored **only on your device** and is removed when you delete the chart, uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **No backup.** The app is excluded from Android backup and from device-to-device transfer: your charts are not copied to your Google account. For the same reason they are **not restored** when you change phones or reinstall the app; to move them to another device, use "Send chart".
- **Local settings.** Your language, theme, display time zone and the calculation options you choose (visible points, node type, house system), also only on your device.
- **Time and time zone.** The app uses your device clock and time zone to show the date; they are not sent anywhere.
- **City list bundled in the app.** To pick a birth place, the app ships a list of cities and time zones from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) (CC BY 4.0 licence) and the IANA time zone database. They are queried on your device; what you search for is not sent anywhere.

## What you choose to send
None of this happens by itself: it always starts with a button you press.

- **"Ask an AI" and "Share data".** They prepare a text with planetary positions and aspects, which can include those of your charts, to copy or send to another app. In "Ask an AI" charts appear as "Person A/B" unless you choose "Real names". In "Share data" the names you saved are used. **This text never includes the birth date or place.**
- **"Send chart".** It prepares a message with a code that lets another person open the chart in their app. **The code contains the chart's data:** the name, the birth date and time, and the place (name, country, coordinates and time zone). The code is compressed, **not encrypted**: whoever receives the message can read that data. Send it only to people you want to have it.
- **Direct send to other apps.** You can send the text straight to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, or a chart message to WhatsApp, if you have those apps installed, or use the system share menu. From there the content is handled by the app you chose, under its own privacy policy; AstroClock is not involved and receives nothing back. To offer those buttons, the app checks whether those four apps are installed; that information is not stored or sent.
- **"Import chart".** When you open it, the app reads the text on your clipboard to paste the code you copied. That text is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere. Before anything is saved you are shown the received chart and you decide.
- **Other people's data.** If you save or send another person's chart, make sure you have their consent.

## Permissions
The app requests no special system permissions and has no internet access.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If data handling ever changes, this page and the date above will be updated before the update is published.
