# Privacy Policy — AstroClock

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

**AstroClock** ("the app") does not collect, upload to servers, share or sell personal data.

## What the app does with your data
- **No data collection.** The app asks for no account, email, name or location.
- **Fully offline.** All planetary-position calculations run on your device. The app does not connect to any server.
- **No analytics, ads or trackers.** It includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **Local settings.** The only preferences stored are your language (System, Español or English), theme (System, Light or Dark) and display time zone (Device, UTC or a fixed offset), kept on your device only. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Time and time zone.** The app uses your device clock and time zone to show the date; they are not sent anywhere.

- **Sharing is your action.** The "Ask an AI" and "Share data" buttons copy, or send to another app you choose, a text with planetary positions and aspects (no personal data). It only happens when you ask; the app sends nothing by itself.

## Permissions
The app requests no special system permissions.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If data handling ever changes, this page and the date above will be updated before the update is published.
