# Privacy Policy — AstroClock

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

**AstroClock** ("the app") does not collect, upload to servers, share or sell personal data.

## What the app does with your data
- **No data collection.** The app asks for no account or email, and does not access your location.
- **Fully offline.** All calculations run on your device. The app does not connect to any server.
- **No analytics, ads or trackers.** It includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- **Charts you enter.** If you create natal charts, the app stores what you type: a name (any name), the birth date, time and place, and whether one of them is "your chart". They can belong to other people. This data is stored **only on your device**, is not sent anywhere, and is removed when you delete the chart, uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Local settings.** Your language (System, Español or English), theme (System, Light or Dark) and display time zone (Device, UTC or a fixed offset), also only on your device.
- **Time and time zone.** The app uses your device clock and time zone to show the date; they are not sent anywhere.
- **City list bundled in the app.** To pick a birth place offline, the app ships a list of cities and time zones from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) (CC BY 4.0 licence) and the IANA time zone database. They are queried on your device; what you search for is not sent anywhere.
- **Sharing is your action.** The "Ask an AI" and "Share data" buttons copy, or send to another app you choose, a text with planetary positions and aspects, which can include those of your charts. In "Ask an AI" charts appear as "Person A/B" unless you choose "Real names"; **birth date and place are never included**. In "Share data" the names you saved are used. It only happens when you ask; the app sends nothing by itself. If you share data about another person, make sure you have their consent.

## Permissions
The app requests no special system permissions.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If data handling ever changes, this page and the date above will be updated before the update is published.
